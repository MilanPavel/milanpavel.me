2026-10-14

Every coder has a page like this somewhere. A plain file. A name. A few links. This one is mine.

I wanted it small. No framework. No build step. One HTML file, one stylesheet, a monospace font. It loads before you notice. It will still load in ten years. Most of the web forgot that this is possible.

Lately I spend my evenings on Golem. It is a personal agent that runs on my machine. You hand it a task and step away. It keeps working. It asks before it does anything that matters. The name comes from the Prague legend: a clay servant, brought to life to work on its maker's behalf. I walk past the old synagogue where he is said to sleep. It felt right.

Building an agent teaches you humility. The model is the easy part. The hard part is everything around it. What happens when the process crashes? When the laptop sleeps? When the task takes all day and you come back to read what it did? Those questions are boring. They are also the whole product.

So this is where I will write about it. What works. What breaks. What I learn along the way. Short posts. Rarely. Only when there is something worth saying.

Thanks for reading.
